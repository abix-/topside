# Deep Stone

## Where it came from

Abarrach, the world of stone, in the Death Gate Cycle (Weis and
Hickman).

## The world

Solid rock full of tunnels, volcanic at the core and frozen at the
edge, livable only in the band between: a lava sea and poison air. See
design.md, "The generators we want".

## The theme

A people who would rather keep their dead than let them go.

## The physics

Heat and bad air as the pressures.

## The logic

The dead are raised to work, and they are a faction.

## Danger

Deadly. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first body that gets up and goes back to work.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever. Nobody Dies sits close to it.
