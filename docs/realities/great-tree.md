# Great Tree

## Where it came from

Elden Ring's Leyndell, Royal Capital, "located at the foot of the
Erdtree", later burned to Leyndell, Ashen Capital (Fextralife wiki, read
2026-09-27). The healing light is ours: the wiki says nothing of the
tree's light healing.

## The world

A city under a tree so big it fills the sky.

## The theme

Faith: the people worship the tree, and someone wants to burn it.

## The physics

The tree's light heals.

## The logic

What the people believe about the tree decides who they follow.

## Danger

Calm. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

When their wounds close in its light.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
