# Mirror

## Where it came from

Rick and Morty, "Close Rick-counters of the Rick Kind": the Council of
Ricks, and a world full of other versions of the same people.

## The world

Like the world the player left, with a second bunker for every bunker
that crossed.

## The theme

Someone else is living your life, maybe better.

## The physics

Normal.

## The logic

Everyone has a double (realities.md, "The rules a reality can change").

## Danger

Hard: an attack can come from the player's own double's bunker, at full
strength. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first time they see themselves.

## The episodes that fit it

The Other You. The Fever.
