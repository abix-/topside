# Sky Islands

## Where it came from

Arianus, the world of air, in the Death Gate Cycle (Weis and Hickman).

## The world

Floating islands at three heights, with a storm that never ends filling
the bottom. One machine the size of a continent makes the water. See
design.md, "The generators we want".

## The theme

Scarcity and power: whoever holds the machine holds everyone.

## The physics

Falling is death. Crossing between islands needs a vehicle.

## The logic

The storm lives below you instead of coming over you.

## Danger

Hard. A boss can knock the player off the edge. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first time the ground ends at sky.

## The episodes that fit it

The Tap (water is scarce). The Fever.
