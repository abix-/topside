# Still Storm

## Where it came from

Elden Ring's Crumbling Farum Azula: floating ruins of the Ancient
Dragons' city, crumbling but never falling, with time frozen and "the
storm beyond time" at its centre, where the Dragonlord Placidusax waits
(Fextralife wiki and web search, read 2026-09-27).

## The world

Ruins floating at the edge of time, inside our own storm, frozen in
place.

## The theme

The storm, stopped: the thing that moves everyone, caught.

## The physics

Time does not move. Things fall toward the ruins.

## The logic

The storm never comes, so this reality does not end on its own.

## Danger

Deadly. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

When the storm never comes.

## The episodes that fit it

The Question. The Fever.
