# Frozen Sea

## Where it came from

Chelestra, the world of water, in the Death Gate Cycle (Weis and
Hickman).

## The world

An egg of ice with a sun moving through it, thawing and freezing the
floating islands people live on as it passes. Boats. See design.md,
"The generators we want".

## The theme

A world that changes while you are in it.

## The physics

The sun's path thaws and freezes the map on a clock.

## The logic

Water kills power: wet guns and tools stop working.

## Danger

Hard. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first time they swim, and their gun will not fire.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
