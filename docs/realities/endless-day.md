# Endless Day

## Where it came from

Pryan, the world of fire, in the Death Gate Cycle (Weis and Hickman).

## The world

A hollow world lit from inside by small suns, covered in jungle miles
high. People live in the canopy; almost nobody has seen the ground. See
design.md, "The generators we want".

## The theme

Too much: too much light, too much growth, nowhere dark to hide.

## The physics

There is no night.

## The logic

Giants roam, asking a question and killing whoever cannot answer.

## Danger

Deadly: a giant is bigger than anything else and not hostile until you
fail its question. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first night that never comes.

## The episodes that fit it

The Question (its giants are this reality's). The Fever.
