# Rot

## Where it came from

Elden Ring's Caelid, "a vast land consummately marred by scarlet rot",
the place of the last battle between General Radahn and Malenia, with
bosses such as Starscourge Radahn and Elder Dragon Greyoll (Fextralife
wiki, read 2026-09-27).

## The world

Red sky, red ground, and ruins of what the rot took.

## The theme

Something terrible happened here, and nobody says what.

## The physics

Normal.

## The logic

A sickness in the soil spreads to anything that stays too long.

## Danger

Deadly: giant bosses and great loot. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first time they stand still too long.

## The episodes that fit it

The Question. The Fever.
