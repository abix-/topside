# Backrooms

The name comes from the 2019 creepypasta and needs one of our own.

## Where it came from

The Backrooms creepypasta (design.md, "The generators we want").

## The world

No sky and no terrain, only rooms: halls, rooms, and openings rolled
forever around the people. Monuments are the places that break the
pattern (a flooded hall, an office, a pool, a parking deck). Minor
sites are an odd door or a hum that changes. See design.md.

## The theme

Liminal dread: fluorescent hum, damp carpet, every hall the same until
it is not.

## The physics

Normal.

## The logic

The map is useless. Something wandering has already heard you.

## Danger

Hard. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first time the bunker door opens onto a hall instead of the sky.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
