# False Stars

## Where it came from

Elden Ring's Siofra River and Nokron, Eternal City (Fextralife wiki,
read 2026-09-27): underground, with "a sky seemingly filled with stars"
a player "could mistake for an overland area". Nokron is a ruin of one
of the Eternal Cities, a people who have vanished. Its bosses include
the Mimic Tear, which becomes a copy of the player.

## The world

A river deep underground, the ruins of a city whose people vanished,
and a sky of stars that are not stars.

## The theme

A night sky where there should be none, and nobody knows who made it
or where its people went.

## The physics

The sky is a ceiling.

## The logic

Normal. No rule from realities.md.

## Danger

Hard. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

When they climb toward the stars and hit rock.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
