# Maze

## Where it came from

The Labyrinth, in the Death Gate Cycle (Weis and Hickman), with
STALKER's anomalies. Mined in design.md, "Maze, mined".

## The world

10 m corridors with walls as high as they are wide, paths and dead
ends, gates toward the centre, and the bunker in a clearing the maze
grows out of. See design.md, "Maze, mined".

## The theme

Chaos against order, the game's own theme, made into a map.

## The physics

The walls move: runs of wall vanish and new ones stand.

## The logic

The maze hates you and sends its creatures. People either keep moving
(runners) or hold a place (squatters).

## Danger

Deadly: the maze sends the boss and moves the walls to help it.
Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

A corridor they walked is not there on the way back.

## The episodes that fit it

The Question. The Fever.
