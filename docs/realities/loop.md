# Loop

## Where it came from

The Death Gate Cycle's lazars (Fire Sea), caught between life and death;
Elden Ring, where death sends the player back (not looked up); and Rick
and Morty, where the people know what they are in.

## The world

Any world. What is different is death.

## The theme

People who know they are stuck in a loop of life and death and never
chose it.

## The physics

Normal.

## The logic

Nobody dies (realities.md, "The rules a reality can change").

## Danger

Deadly: the player dies and comes back, so a boss can be learned.
Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

Seeing someone they know to be dead, or their own first death.

## The episodes that fit it

Nobody Dies. The Question: the player can die, learn the boss, and try
again. The Fever.
