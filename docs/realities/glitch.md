# Glitch

## Where it came from

Rick and Morty, "M. Night Shaym-Aliens!": simulations inside
simulations, built to get something out of the one trapped in them.

## The world

A world that looks normal and is a trap built to get something out of
the player.

## The theme

Nothing is real, and someone is watching what you do.

## The physics

It glitches: the same tree twice, a wall a step out of place.

## The logic

People repeat themselves. The world wants something from the player.

## Danger

Calm on the surface; the danger is the trap. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first glitch.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
