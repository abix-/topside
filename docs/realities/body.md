# Body

## Where it came from

Rick and Morty, "Anatomy Park": a theme park inside a man's body, and
the diseases get loose.

## The world

The world is inside something alive: the ground is its flesh, the rivers
its blood.

## The theme

You live inside something that does not know you are there, yet.

## The physics

The weather is its breathing, the ground heaves with its pulse.

## The logic

Diseases are the monsters. If it gets sick, the world gets worse; if it
dies, so does the world.

## Danger

Deadly. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

When the ground heaves.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
