# Ruined

## Where it came from

Rick and Morty, "Rick Potion #9": a world ruined by a fix that went
wrong, where the family ends up happy.

## The world

A world someone else already destroyed, trying to fix something. The
buildings stand; the people are what is left.

## The theme

Someone else's disaster, and the people who made a life in it anyway.

## The physics

Normal.

## The logic

The people here used to be someone. Some of them are happy as they are,
and do not want saving.

## Danger

Hard. Proposed, not decided.

## How the player finds out

The first person they meet who used to be someone.

## The episodes that fit it

The Fever.
