# Nobody Dies

The fourth episode (operator, 2026-09-27). It runs in a reality with the
rule "nobody dies" (realities.md): death does not stick, and the people
know they are stuck in the loop and never chose it. It is about games
themselves: in most games people die and come back
without knowing; here they know, and they talk about it.

## Parameters

- The realities it fits: any reality with the rule "nobody dies"
  (realities.md). The player finds out from the one at the door, someone
  they know to be dead (pivot point 1).
- Who comes back: everyone, only the player's bunker, only the people
  the player has killed, or only one person.
- What each return costs: a memory, their face, their loyalty, or a
  little of their mind. It adds up.
- Who pays for it: the one who returns, the one who killed them, or
  someone in another bunker who dies for good.
- The one at the door: always a bunker person, from a random bunker, a
  random person in it (operator, 2026-09-27). Topside people are gone for
  good after each episode, so only a bunker person can come back across
  episodes.
- Who knows it is a loop: only the dead, everyone, or one person who
  has worked it out and cannot make anyone believe them.
- The reveal: one of the four under "The reveal".

## The ordinary problem

Grief and guilt: what you would do if you could take back a death, and
what you owe the people you hurt.

## The people

- The one at the door: a random person from a random bunker, by the
  parameter. They already exist, so they walk from their bunker to the
  player's door; nobody is placed there.
- The one who comes back wrong: someone in the player's bunker who has
  died too many times.
- The one killing on purpose: someone who uses the loop as a punishment
  that never ends.
- The ones who want out: people who have come back too many times.
- Mara's bunker.

## How it opens

Someone the player lost in an earlier episode knocks on the bunker door:
"You let me die."

## The wish

Nobody you love ever has to be gone again.

## Pushed all the way

- Nobody is afraid any more. The player's people pick fights and take
  stupid risks.
- Mara's bunker works it out too. Raids never end, because the dead come
  back and raid again.
- The dead pile up grudges. Everyone remembers every death, and goes
  after whoever killed them.
- Someone starts killing on purpose, as a punishment that never ends.
- The player dies and comes back too, with no loot. Nobody keeps
  anything.
- The people know they are stuck. Some want out and ask to stay dead.
  Some blame the player: "It started when you got here." Some treat the
  player as the only one who can end it.

## The second story at home

Someone in the player's bunker has died too many times and is not
themselves any more. They wander, forget the player's name, and one day
do not know whose side they are on.

## The reveal

One of these, by the parameter:

- Every return kills someone else for good, somewhere the player cannot
  see. Every coming back is a trade.
- The ones who come back are not the same people. They are copies, and
  each copy is worse.
- After the next storm nobody comes back. This is the last reality where
  it works.
- It is only the player. The loop follows the player; everyone else is
  trapped in it because the player is here.

## The real cost

The people the player brought back are losing themselves, and someone
else is paying for it.

## The pivot points

1. The one at the door. Remembered by them.
   - Take them in.
   - Send them away.
   - Kill them again to see if it is true.
2. Telling your people. Remembered by the player's bunker.
   - Tell them death does not stick.
   - Keep it secret and watch them find out.
3. The one who comes back wrong. Remembered by them, whatever is left of
   them.
   - Look after them.
   - Shut them away.
   - Let them go.
4. Someone asks the player to end their loop. Remembered by them and by
   everyone who hears of it.
   - Do it.
   - Refuse.
   - Promise, and do not.
5. The raids. Remembered by Mara's bunker.
   - Fight them forever.
   - Make peace with Mara's people.
6. The one killing on purpose. Remembered by whoever they killed.
   - Stop them.
   - Join them.
   - Turn them loose on your enemies.
7. The player's own first death. Remembered by the player's killer.
   - Find out who did it and go after them.
   - Let it go.
8. The reveal, found only by following someone who returns, finding the
   ones dying for good, or dying enough times to see the pattern.
9. The last choice before the storm. Each plays out through the eight
   before it.
   - Stop the returns for everyone: everyone's next death is final.
   - Keep them going and let the cost pile up.
   - Save one person and let the rest go.
   - Use it: end your enemies the only way that sticks.

## The endings

- Stop it: everyone gets one death. The people who owed the player
  their returns remember that the player took them away; the ones who
  wanted out remember that the player freed them.
- Keep it: the player's people live forever, and are less and less
  themselves.
- Save one: the one chosen lives. Everyone else knows the player chose.
- Use it: the player's enemies are gone for good, and everyone knows how.

## What carries across the storm

Every death and who did it stays in memory. People carry grudges into
the next reality over things that did not stick, murders the victims
came back from included.

## What the game does not do yet that this episode needs

Not checked against the code yet: a person coming back after death with
their memory, a return that costs something, people talking about the
loop, talking to people, and people remembering who killed them.
design.md has the player die for good and keep nothing; this episode
changes the first of those for one reality only.
