# The Tap

The first episode (operator, 2026-09-27). It starts when the player
wakes in the bunker knowing nothing.

## Parameters

- The realities it fits: any reality where water is scarce
  (realities.md).
- The endless thing: water, food, power, or ammo.
- What it drains: a well, someone's memory, someone's life, or the
  ground under a building.
- Who pays: Mara's bunker, the player's own bunker, or a stranger.
- Who tells the player about it: Dell or someone else.

The rest of this file is written with the first of each: water, a well,
Mara's bunker, Dell, and a reality with the rule "water remembers"
(realities.md). The player finds out with the first drink at the tap
(pivot point 2).

## The ordinary problem

There is not enough water, for you or for your neighbours.

## The people

- Dell: a person in the player's bunker who hoards, afraid there will
  never be enough.
- Mara: the leader of the bunker over the hill. Mara's youngest is
  there too.
- The strangers: people from further off who come once word of the
  water travels.

Dell and Mara are the names used here; in the game each part is filled
by a person who already exists.

## How it opens

The bunker's water store is low and the people are thirsty. Dell tells
the player about a tap in a ruin down the road that still runs: clean
water that never stops.

## The wish

The tap is real. Water without end, the one thing everyone needs.

## Pushed all the way

- Mara's people find the tap the same day.
- Dell wants it kept secret and starts storing far more water than the
  bunker can drink.
- Mara's people camp at the tap, then guard it.
- Word travels. Strangers come, and the ruin fills with people who all
  want the same thing.
- Everyone who drinks sees what the drinker before them saw. Nothing
  done near the tap stays secret for long: whoever drinks after the
  player may see what the player did.

## The second story at home

Dell trades the extra water to strangers behind the player's back, for
food and ammo, and makes friends the player does not know. Whoever
drinks the stored water sees Dell's trades.

## The reveal

The tap does not make water. It draws it from the ground under Mara's
bunker. Every bucket taken at the tap is one Mara's people lose, and
their well is drying up. Nobody tells the player. They find out by
going to Mara's bunker, finding the dry well, hearing it from one of
Mara's people, or drinking after one of Mara's people and seeing it.

## The real cost

Mara's youngest is sick with thirst. The water that saves the player's
people is killing someone else's.

## The pivot points

1. Dell tells you about the tap. Remembered by Dell.
   - Believe Dell and go: the episode starts at the tap.
   - Do not believe it and look for water elsewhere: Mara's people
     find the tap first, and you arrive to a place already guarded.
   - Send Dell to check: Dell goes alone and comes back with a story
     you cannot be sure of.
2. The first drink at the tap: the player finds out what is different.
   Remembered by whoever drinks after.
   - Drink: you see what the last drinker saw, perhaps one of Mara's
     people and their thirst.
   - Carry it home and let someone else drink first: they see it, and
     you learn it only from what they tell you.
   - Do not drink: you have water and know nothing more.
3. Who you take. Remembered by Dell and whoever goes.
   - Alone: quiet, and weak.
   - Dell: Dell watches everything you do and wants the secret kept.
   - Someone else: Dell stays home unwatched, and the trading starts
     sooner.
4. The first meeting with Mara's people at the tap. Remembered by
   Mara's bunker.
   - Talk: you learn their names, their thirst, and that Mara has a
     child.
   - Hide and watch: you learn how many they are and when they drink.
   - Shoot first: there is no talking to Mara's bunker again this
     episode.
   - Leave: the tap is theirs for now.
5. What you tell your own people. Remembered by the player's bunker.
   - Tell everyone: the whole bunker wants a share, and some go on
     their own.
   - Tell only Dell: the secret stays small, and Dell holds it over you.
   - Tell no one: you carry water home and have to explain where it
     came from, unless someone drinks it and sees.
6. Dell's storing is noticed, only if the player checks the store or
   drinks from it. Remembered by Dell.
   - Stop Dell: Dell resents you, and may leave.
   - Let it go on: the store fills, and strangers come to your door.
   - Take Dell's side: you are in the trade, and get food and ammo.
7. The strangers arrive. Remembered by the strangers.
   - Trade with them: goods, and the word spreads further, so more come.
   - Send them away: some leave; some tell others where the water is.
   - Point them at Mara's people: the strangers and Mara fight, and you
     pay nothing for it.
   - Take one in: a new person in your bunker, one Dell does not trust.
8. Finding out about Mara's well. It can only happen if an earlier
   choice opened it: drinking at pivot point 2, talking at pivot point
   4, going to Mara's bunker, or catching one of Mara's people alone.
   - Never find out: the ending is built on what you did not know.
   - Find out and keep quiet: you are the only one who knows what the
     tap costs, until someone drinks after you.
   - Find out and tell your people: some want to stop, some do not care.
     Remembered by the player's bunker.
9. Mara's child. Remembered by Mara.
   - Bring water to Mara's bunker: Mara trusts you, and your store runs
     lower.
   - Trade water for something Mara has: guns, maps, or people.
   - Do nothing: the child lives or dies by whether the well fills again.
   - Use it: water for Mara's loyalty, and nothing without it.
10. The fight for the tap. It starts whether or not the player is there.
    Remembered by everyone in it.
    - Join Mara: you hold the tap together.
    - Join the strangers: new friends, and Mara as an enemy.
    - Fight everyone: you hold the tap alone, if you live.
    - Stay out of it: you go home to whatever Dell has been doing.
11. The last choice before the storm: share the tap, hold it, break it,
    tell everyone the truth, or walk away. Each plays out through the
    ten before it: who trusts you, who is alive, whether Dell is still
    home, whether you learned the cost.

## The endings

- Share it: a fragile peace with Mara. Dell hates it and may leave for
  the strangers.
- Hold it: you drive Mara's people off and have water. Mara's bunker
  remembers.
- Break it: Mara's well fills again. Your people are thirsty and angry
  with you. Mara's people remember you did it for them.
- Tell everyone the truth: nobody wants to hear it. The strangers call
  you a liar and keep drinking.
- Walk away: Mara's people take the tap and grow strong. Dell joins
  them.

## What carries across the storm

The storm comes and destroys everything, the tap with it. Every choice
about the tap and the water stops mattering. The surprise is that what
the player did to people is all that is left: Mara, Dell, the strangers,
and everything they remember. This teaches the player in the first
episode that things do not last and people do.

The next episode is cast from it: Mara as a friend, an enemy, or someone
who owes you; Dell at home or with the strangers.

## What the game does not do yet that this episode needs

Not checked against the code yet: talking to people, trading, people
remembering a particular thing the player did, checking the bunker's
store, people guarding a place, and water that carries one person's
memories to another.
