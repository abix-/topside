# The Question

The fifth episode (operator, 2026-09-27). A boss defense episode
(episodes.md, "Boss episodes and foreboding"), from the giants of
Endless Day, who ask a question and kill whoever cannot answer.

## Parameters

- The realities it fits: deadly ones: Endless Day, Rot, Still Storm,
  Maze, Loop (realities.md).
- The boss: a giant, a swarm, the player's double's army, or the maze
  itself.
- What stops it: an answer, a sacrifice, a weakness, or nothing.
- Who it wants: filled from what people remember of the episodes
  before.
- Danger: by the reality.

The rest of this file is written with the first of each: a giant,
stopped by an answer.

## The signs, in the episode before

- Something huge walking on the horizon at night.
- A bunker found emptied, the same words scratched on every wall.
- Strangers fleeing past the door, and none of them will say what they
  saw.

## The ordinary problem

Protecting your home, and what you would give up to do it.

## The people

- The giant.
- The one it wants: someone in the player's bunker, maybe the player.
- Mara's bunker, as allies or not, by what they remember.
- The survivors: people who heard the question and lived.

## How it opens

The storm lands and the ground shakes. It is coming toward the
player's bunker.

## The wish

To be left alone. And behind the giant, the best loot in the reality.

## Pushed all the way

- It asks one question at every door it reaches. If nobody answers
  right, everyone inside dies.
- Each night it is closer.
- It empties another bunker, maybe Mara's.

## The second story at home

The player's people panic. Some want to run, some want to dig deeper.

## The reveal

The question is about someone in the player's bunker: "Where is the one
who ___?" The blank is filled from what people remember: who broke the
tap, who brought the fever, who killed the double. It is hunting one of
the player's people, and it might be the player.

## The real cost

The player can save everyone by giving up one person.

## The pivot points

1. The signs, in the episode before. Remembered by the player's
   bunker.
   - Get ready: store ammo, dig deeper, make allies.
   - Run early.
   - Ignore them.
2. Finding out what it asks. Remembered by whoever the player learns it
   from.
   - Hear it from the survivors.
   - Go and watch it at another bunker's door.
   - Wait until it reaches yours.
3. Allies. Remembered by Mara's bunker.
   - Ask Mara's bunker for help: who comes is what they remember.
   - Stand alone.
4. The emptied bunker. Remembered by anyone who survived it.
   - Search it: loot, and somewhere in it, the answer.
   - Stay away.
5. Finding out who it wants. Remembered by the player's bunker.
   - Tell your people.
   - Keep it quiet.
6. The one it wants. Remembered by them and by everyone who sees.
   - Protect them.
   - Hand them over.
   - Let them run.
   - If it is the player: go out and face it.
7. The night it reaches the door. Each plays out through the six before
   it.
   - Answer it, if the player learned how.
   - Fight it: crazy hard, possible only with allies, or in Loop, where
     the player can die, learn it, and try again.
   - Hand over the one it wants.
   - Send it on to Mara's bunker.
   - Abandon the bunker.

## The endings

- Answered: it leaves, and everyone knows the player knew.
- Killed: the best loot in the reality, and the people who fought beside
  the player remember it forever.
- Handed over: the player's bunker lives, and nobody forgets who the
  player gave up.
- Sent to Mara: the player lives, and Mara's bunker does not.
- Ran: the bunker is lost, and the people are not.

## What carries across the storm

Who stood with the player, who the player gave up or saved, whether the
player sent it to Mara, and whether the giant still lives to come back.

## What the game does not do yet that this episode needs

Not checked against the code yet: a boss far bigger than a person, an
actor that asks a question and hears an answer, a bunker emptied by
something, the signs planted in the episode before, allies coming to
defend a bunker, talking to people, and people remembering a particular
thing the player did.
