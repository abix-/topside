# The Fever

The second episode (operator, 2026-09-27). It follows The Tap. A
different kind of story from The Tap: that one goes out to get
something; this one happens at home, and someone is blamed.

## Parameters

- The realities it fits: any (realities.md).
- The sickness: a fever, or any sickness that spreads between people.
- Who brings the cure: a travelling doctor, a machine, or a person whose
  blood cures it.
- Where it came from: by what the player let in during The Tap (see
  "The reveal").
- Who is blamed: the player's bunker, by Mara's bunker, or Mara's
  bunker, by the player's people.

The rest of this file is written with the first of each: a fever, Sil
the doctor, the player's bunker blamed, and a reality with the rule "the
sick share minds" (realities.md). The player finds out by catching it
(pivot point 2).

## The ordinary problem

Someone you care about is sick, and there is not enough medicine for
everyone.

## The people

Cast from what people remember of The Tap (built so far, orchestrator,
2026-10-03: the first sick person, the one of the player's bunker who
feels most toward the player; Dell, whoever played Dell, only if still of
the player's bunker; Mara, whoever played Mara; Sil and the hidden sick
person are this episode's own work, later):

- The first sick person: whoever in the player's bunker trusted the
  player most in The Tap.
- Dell: in it only if Dell stayed home.
- Mara: the leader of the bunker over the hill. Comes as a friend asking
  for help or as an enemy coming to take, by how The Tap ended.
- Sil: a travelling doctor, new in this episode.
- The hidden sick person: one of the player's people, afraid of being
  shut away or passed over.

## How it opens

After the storm, in the new world, one of the player's people has a
fever. The next morning two more have it.

## The wish

Sil arrives with medicine that works. One dose, one cure.

## Pushed all the way

- There are only a few doses, and Sil's price rises with each one.
- The fever reaches Mara's bunker, and Mara's people say the player's
  bunker brought it.
- The player's healthy people fight over who gets the next dose.
- Everyone with the fever hears what the others think, in both bunkers.
  Secrets stop being secret among the sick.
- A dose cures the fever and cuts the cured one off from the others'
  thoughts. Some of the sick do not want to be cured.

## The second story at home

One of the player's people is sick and hiding it, afraid of being shut
away or passed over for a dose. The other sick can hear them.

## The reveal

The fever came from what the player let in during The Tap:

- If Dell traded with the strangers, it came from them.
- If the player took a stranger in, it came from that stranger.
- If neither, it came from Sil's own camp: Sil sells the sickness and
  the cure. Sil never catches it, and none of the sick can hear Sil.

So Mara's people are right, or wrong, by what the player did last
episode.

## The real cost

There are not enough doses. Someone does not get one, and the player
chooses who.

## The pivot points

1. The first sick person. Remembered by the sick person and the bunker.
   - Shut them away: the fever spreads slower, and they remember being
     shut away.
   - Keep them with everyone: the fever spreads faster.
   - Send them out: they may die out there, or come back with someone.
2. Catching it: the player finds out what is different. Remembered by
   every sick person who hears the player.
   - Stay near the sick and catch it: you hear every sick person's
     thoughts, in both bunkers, and they hear yours.
   - Keep away: you stay well, and learn only what the sick tell you.
3. Sil's price. Remembered by Sil.
   - Pay: doses, and the price rises for the next.
   - Bargain: fewer doses, and Sil stays longer.
   - Rob Sil: the doses, but none of Sil's knowledge of where the fever
     came from.
   - Send Sil away: no doses.
4. The first dose. Remembered by everyone who did not get it.
   - The first sick person.
   - Yourself, which cuts you off from the others' thoughts.
   - Someone useful, like the one who guards the door.
5. Mara's accusation. Remembered by Mara's bunker.
   - Deny it: if you are sick, Mara's sick hear whether you believe it.
   - Admit it.
   - Blame the strangers or Sil.
   - Offer Mara doses.
6. The hidden sick person, found by looking, or by hearing them if the
   player is sick. Remembered by the hidden sick person and the bunker.
   - Expose them.
   - Protect them.
   - Keep their secret and watch the fever spread.
7. The source. Remembered by whoever the trail leads to.
   - Trace the sickness back, by following the sick or by listening
     for the one who brought it: it leads to the reveal.
   - Never look: the ending is built on what you did not know.
8. Sil's last doses. Remembered by Mara and by the player's bunker.
   - Mara's child.
   - One of your own.
9. The last choice before the storm. Each plays out through the eight
   before it.
   - Settle with Mara.
   - Break with Mara.
   - Deal with Sil: keep Sil, drive Sil off, or turn Sil over to Mara.

## The endings

- Cure your own: your bunker lives. Mara's bunker lost people and
  blames you.
- Share with Mara: fewer of yours are cured. Mara owes you.
- Expose Sil: Mara's people take revenge on Sil, and now nobody has
  doses.
- Admit the truth, if the fever came from your side: Mara never forgets
  it, as respect or as revenge.
- Do nothing: the fever takes whoever it takes, and your people remember
  you did not choose.

## What carries across the storm

Who the player saved, who they passed over, whether Mara thinks they
brought the sickness, whether Sil is alive to come back, and what the
sick heard each other think.

## What the game does not do yet that this episode needs

Not checked against the code yet: sickness that spreads between people,
medicine, shutting someone away, a person hiding that they are sick,
the sick hearing each other's thoughts, talking to people, trading, and
people remembering a particular thing the player did.
