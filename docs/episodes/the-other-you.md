# The Other You

The third episode (operator, 2026-09-27). The storm shifts people
between realities (design.md, "The story"), so this reality already
has a you: a double with their own bunker, their own Dell, their own
Mara, who made the opposite of every choice the player made.

## Parameters

- The realities it fits: any reality with the rule "everyone has a
  double" (realities.md). The player finds out by seeing their double
  (pivot point 1).
- Who has a double: the player, Dell, Mara, or the player's whole
  bunker.
- What the double did differently: the opposite of the player's own
  remembered choices in the episodes before, so every player gets a
  double from their own past.
- What the double wants: the player's bunker, the player's people, the
  player's life, or only to warn them.
- What the double is like: better than the player, worse, or a mirror.
- The reveal: one of the four under "The reveal".

## The ordinary problem

Someone else is living your life better than you (Rick and Morty,
Rixty Minutes).

## The people

- The double: the player's other self, in this reality.
- Dell and the other Dell.
- Mara, as friend or enemy by the episodes before, who cannot tell the
  player and the double apart.
- The player's people, and the double's people.

## How it opens

One of the player's people comes back from the road white in the face:
"I saw you. Out there. You were someone else's."

## The wish

The double's bunker is thriving: water, medicine, loyal people, because
their choices worked. The double wants to meet.

## Pushed all the way

- The double offers to merge the two bunkers.
- The player's people start to like the double better.
- Dell meets the other Dell.
- Mara treats the player by what the double did to her.
- The player's own people start asking which one is the real one.

## The second story at home

The player's people are quietly holding a vote on which of the two to
follow.

## The reveal

One of these, by the parameter:

- The double is the original. The player is the copy the storm made.
- The double's bunker thrives on something terrible: a tap that drains
  someone else, the way the tap drained Mara.
- There are more than two, and a third is coming.
- The double's reality ends at the next storm, and they want the
  player's life to escape it.

## The real cost

Only one of the two leaves with the storm.

## The pivot points

1. The first sighting. Remembered by the double.
   - Go and meet the double.
   - Spy on them.
   - Hide from them.
2. The meeting. Remembered by the double and by Mara.
   - Tell the truth about your choices.
   - Lie, and claim theirs.
3. Mara mistakes you for the double. Remembered by Mara.
   - Play along and take the double's friendship or debt.
   - Correct her.
4. The two Dells. Remembered by both Dells.
   - Keep yours away from theirs.
   - Let them meet: they may compare what they know about you.
5. The merge offer. Remembered by both bunkers.
   - Agree.
   - Refuse.
   - Pretend to agree.
6. The vote at home. Remembered by the player's people.
   - Campaign for yourself.
   - Let it happen.
   - Stop it.
7. The reveal, found only by getting inside the double's bunker:
   invited, sneaking in, or through the other Dell. Remembered by
   whoever catches you.
8. The last choice before the storm. Each plays out through the seven
   before it.
   - Kill the double.
   - Swap places: everyone is equal, so the player can become the double
     and keep their people, their memories and their friends.
   - Merge and follow them.
   - Warn them and leave.
   - Let the storm decide.

## The endings

- Kill them: one of you is left, and everyone who knew the double
  remembers who did it.
- Become them: the player carries the double's past, and people treat
  them by what the double did.
- Follow them: the player's bunker becomes the double's.
- Save them: the double lives and owes the player.
- Run: the double is left to whatever the reveal said.

## What carries across the storm

Whichever of the two remains, and everyone's memory of which one they
thought the player was.

## What the game does not do yet that this episode needs

Not checked against the code yet: a second copy of a person with their
own memories, the player taking another person's body, a bunker vote,
talking to people, and people remembering a particular thing the player
did.
